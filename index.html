Tikroo for Google Classroom

Tikroo brings live quizzes, polls, and interactive learning activities into the classroom. This domain hosts the developer and testing environment for the Tikroo add-on for Google Classroom.

Quizzes & polls Self-paced assignments Classroom grade sync Roster-aware

Teachers attach a Tikroo activity to an assignment; students play inside Classroom and their scores return to the gradebook.
